Who I Am

About Melissa

I came to tarot in 2016 during a hard season of my life. What started as simply something new to learn ended up becoming one of the most powerful tools for healing and clarity I've ever found.

My approach is anything but traditional. I don't work from fixed spreads — instead, I do free pulls and let spirit paint the picture. I think of the cards like pages in a book. The more cards laid down, the more pages, the more chapters of your story revealed.

Whether you're navigating love, searching for direction, or simply seeking a deeper understanding of where you are — I'm here to help you read your story.
